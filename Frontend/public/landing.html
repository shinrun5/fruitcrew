The weekly schedule your whole crew can actually use.

Fruit Crew is a small, no-fuss staff-scheduling app for small teams. Build the week, post it, and let people swap shifts, check their hours and keep each other in the loop — from their phone.

A small app, on purpose

Fruit Crew started as a tool for a couple of small food shops and grew into what those shops actually needed — nothing more. It's built and run by one person. There's no sales team, no per-seat pricing, and no giant settings screen.

It's invite-only: once your business is set up, you hand out codes to your team and everyone else just logs in. If you run a small place and want to try it, request access below.

What it does

Everything a small team needs to run a week — and not much else.

🗓️ Scheduling

  • Auto-build a draft schedule from availability and staffing needs
  • Drag-free editing — tap a slot, pick a person
  • Publish when it's ready; the team only sees the posted version
  • Per-store hours, per-weekday hours and holidays

🔄 Shift swaps & marketplace

  • Post a shift you can't work and let a coworker claim it
  • Hand off just part of a shift
  • Manager approves every change; can assign one directly
  • No requests for shifts already worked

⏰ Availability & time off

  • Set standing weekly hours once — or override a single week
  • Weekly “my hours are right” confirmation
  • Time-off notices the manager acknowledges
  • Max days / hours and day-preference rules

💬 Chat & shift notes

  • A group chat per store, with @-mentions
  • Private direct messages between coworkers
  • Shift pass-down log — refunds, complaints, lost & found, low stock

📣 Reminders & email

  • Friday nudge to check next week's availability
  • Email when a shift hits the marketplace or you're @-mentioned
  • In-app notifications for everything

📱 Built for phones

  • Works in the browser — nothing to install
  • English or 中文
  • Add it to your home screen like an app
