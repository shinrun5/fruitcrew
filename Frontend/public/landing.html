How it works

Your week, mostly on autopilot

Set it up once. After that, every week runs itself — you just look it over and post.

Once

Tell it what you need

Your opening hours and how many people each shift needs. Share one sign-up link and your crew joins themselves.

Every Friday

Your crew checks their hours

Everyone gets a reminder to confirm next week's availability — or change it just for that week.

Every Saturday

The schedule drafts itself

Fruit Crew builds next week around everyone's availability, hour limits and time off. You tweak and tap Post.

All week

Changes sort themselves out

Can't make a shift? Post it, a coworker claims it, you approve. Everyone's notified.

Features

Everything you need, included

Built for crews whose week never looks the same twice — and not much else.

🗓️ Scheduling

  • Drafts the week from availability, hour limits and staffing needs
  • Tap anyone to change their shifts; see who's short at a glance
  • Your crew only sees what you've posted
  • Store hours, holidays, and sections like front & kitchen

🏠 A home screen that tells you what's next New

  • Swaps to approve, time off, new sign-ups, open shifts — in one list
  • Tap any item to go straight to it
  • A getting-started checklist for your first week

🔄 Swaps & shift marketplace

  • Post a shift you can't work; a coworker claims it
  • Hand off just part of a shift
  • You approve every change, and see who'd go over their hours

⏰ Availability & time off

  • Standing weekly hours, or change just one week
  • Time off the scheduler works around automatically
  • Max hours, max days and day rules per person

💵 Payroll hours New

  • Everyone's hours for the pay period — to the minute, not rounded
  • Weekly, every two weeks or monthly — or any dates you pick
  • Each person's hours on their profile too

👥 One page per person

  • Stores, level, limits, availability and time off in one place
  • Approve new sign-ups with one tap
  • Hire dates and hours worked

💬 Chat & shift notes Add-ons

  • A staff-only group chat per store, with @-mentions
  • Direct messages between coworkers
  • A shift handoff log — refunds, complaints, lost & found

✅ Closing duties Add-on

  • Who closes, and what needs doing before they leave
  • Staff see their own duties; managers export the list

📱 Built for phones

  • Works in any browser — add it to your home screen
  • Your shifts in Apple, Google or Outlook Calendar, always up to date
  • English, 中文 and Español
